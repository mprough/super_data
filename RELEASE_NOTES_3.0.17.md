# SuperData 3.0.17

- Makes Product country-of-origin support work without Google Product Loader.
- Installs and preserves the shared `products_country_of_origin` product field for modern Zen Cart stores.
- Adds a standalone SuperData default-country setting and per-product country selector.
- Defers to Google Product Loader's resolver, default, and product editor when that plugin is installed, preventing duplicate data entry.
