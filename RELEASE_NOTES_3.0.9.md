# SuperData 3.0.9

SuperData 3.0.9 corrects the configuration-page ordering introduced with the five shipping rate tables.

## Fixes

- Keeps every shipping zone together as one five-field block.
- Displays rate type, country, regions, rates, and handling charge in the same order for Zones 1–5.
- Moves Product Condition, Default Product Weight, Out of Stock Status, and the out-of-stock availability delay below the complete shipping section.
- Repairs existing sort orders automatically during a Plugin Manager upgrade from 3.0.8.
- Removes the obsolete Google - Publisher URL setting and its discontinued Google+ publisher link output.
